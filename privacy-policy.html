What this app stores

Birma AI Audit runs primarily in your browser. Workflow definitions, client contact details, audit logs, and analysis results are stored locally in your browser's storage on the device you're using — not on a remote database, unless you are using the version hosted inside claude.ai, in which case data is saved to your Claude account's artifact storage.

Footage and images

Video files you load are processed locally in your browser for playback and manual audit logging. If you use the Auto-Analyze Footage feature, individual sampled frames (still images) are sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) for analysis. Full video files are not uploaded for that feature — only sampled still frames.

Worker monitoring notice

If this tool is used to review footage of employees or contractors for efficiency, performance, or compliance purposes, [Birma Enterprises / client company name] is responsible for ensuring workers are informed and that use complies with applicable local employment, privacy, and video-surveillance laws.

Data you provide

Client contact information (name, email, phone) entered into the Clients section is stored locally as described above and is not transmitted anywhere unless you export or share it yourself.

Cookies and local storage

This app does not use tracking cookies. It uses browser local storage to remember your data between visits. You can decline this when prompted, or clear it anytime via your browser settings.
